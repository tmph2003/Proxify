## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

- **The First Law of Usability**: "Don't make me think!" A web page should be self-evident. Obvious. The user should be able to "get it" without expending effort thinking about it.
- **Satisficing**: Users don't choose the best option; they choose the first reasonable option. 
- **Muddling Through**: Users don't read instructions or figure out how things work. They just use them. If it works, they stick to it.
- **The Trunk Test**: A metric for evaluating navigation. You should be able to be dropped anywhere on a site (like being dropped from a car trunk) and answer: What site is this? What page am I on? What are the major sections? What are my options at this level? Where am I in the scheme of things? How can I search?
- **Usability Testing**: Testing one user is 100% better than testing none. Testing one user early in the project is better than testing 50 near the end.
